Easier way 1
for “Maths online looks lifeless”

Give each idea its own colour, and keep that colour everywhere it appears.

In y = mx + c, if m is blue in the equation, the slope is blue on the graph. If c is orange, the point where the line meets the y-axis is orange too. Students follow the colour and see where each part comes from.

Remember those grey pages from the first moment? Here's the easier way: colour. When each part of an idea has its own colour, and keeps it everywhere, students stop hunting for connections because the colour shows them. Look at this Robogebra lesson. In y equals 2x plus 3, the 2 is blue and the 3 is orange. On the graph, the slope triangle is blue and the point where the line crosses the y-axis is orange. A student doesn't need you to say "the m value gives the slope" three times, because they can see it. Now watch what happens when I change the 2 to a 3. The blue triangle grows and the line gets steeper. The colour carries the link from symbol to picture. You can do this with coloured chalk tomorrow, and a tool like Robogebra keeps the colours matched for you on every line and every graph.

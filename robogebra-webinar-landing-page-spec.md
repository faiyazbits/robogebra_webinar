## 1. Purpose of the page

- Traffic comes from **Meta ads** (image posters + 1-minute video) inviting maths teachers to a **free live webinar**.
- The page has two jobs:
  1. Get the teacher to **fill the registration form**.
  2. Get her to **join The Vertex**, a WhatsApp community of maths teachers across India.
- The webinar gives real value. Robogebra is shown during it, but **nothing is sold**. The page must not read like a sales page.

## 3. Audience

- Maths teachers, **Grades 6–12**
- **All boards, pan-India** (CBSE, ICSE, state boards)
- **Coaching centre maths teachers** are welcome. Mention them **only** in the "Who should join" section. All other copy stays written for school teachers (periods, sections, timetable, school).
- Keep the Indian school system and teacher reality in mind in every line.

---

## 4. Page principles

- **Uncluttered.** Short sections, lots of white space, one idea per section.
- **Mobile-first.** Almost all Meta ad traffic lands on a phone.
- **Message match.** The hero hook and headline must be the same as the poster the teacher clicked.
- **Tone:** we assist teachers and ask for their collaboration and feedback. It must **not** sound like a training workshop or like we're teaching maths to teachers. It must **not** sound like fun or a hangout either.
- **Never blame the teacher.** The problems come with the job.

**Inspiration:** https://viralreelframework.netlify.app/. We borrow its **flow** (hero → details → what you'll get → expert → what happens next → FAQ → final CTA), **not** its urgency devices.

**Leave these out, on purpose:**
- Countdown timer
- Seat counter ("156/200 filled")
- Strike-through prices or a rupee value stack
- "As featured on" logos
- Testimonial carousel (there are no webinar testimonials yet)
- Engagement bait ("Comment YES…")

The only urgency on the page is real: the coupon is for **live attendees only**.
